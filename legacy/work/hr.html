Case study · HR and payroll

payroll on your rules.

Lampara’s crews work across many sites each day. Attendance arrived by message, and payroll was computed by hand from HR’s spreadsheet every cutoff. This module takes attendance from phones and computes payroll the way HR already did.

Client
Lampara Electrical Installation Services
Area
HR, attendance and payroll
Platforms
Web and Android app
Used by
HR, managers, crew leaders, employees

The problem

before.

HR’s spreadsheet held years of rules: cutoffs, time-in rounding, overtime, holiday multipliers, contributions, cash advances. Only HR understood the formulas, and one wrong cell meant a payslip dispute.

The goal: keep the same rules, compute them automatically, and leave the approvals to people.

Who uses it

one system, every role.

  • HR

    Approves requests, runs payroll, manages employees, pay settings and reports.

  • Manager

    Plans manpower per site and reviews timesheets before payroll.

  • Crew leader

    Times in the whole crew from one phone and files team overtime.

  • Employee

    Times in and out on their phone, files overtime, leave and expenses, and downloads payslips.

What was built

a walk through it.

  1. 01

    Today, at a glance

    Who is in, who is late, and who has not timed out yet, grouped by today’s site plan.

    • Needs-attention list for HR

  2. 02

    Plan who goes where

    Build each day’s site plan, compare it with yesterday’s, then publish it to the crews.

    • Copy a plan, move several people at once

  3. 03

    One queue for approvals

    Overtime, time corrections, manual entries, leave and expenses, each approved or rejected with a reason.

    • Expenses can be marked paid in cash

  4. 04

    Timesheet per cutoff

    A grid of every employee and every day in the cutoff, with lates, absences and missing time-outs marked.

    • Review before payroll is computed

  5. 05

    Payroll computed, then approved

    Gross, deductions and net for every employee, from the approved timesheet. Recalculate, export to Excel, then approve.

    • Payslips generated as PDFs

  6. 06

    The pay rules, as settings

    Rest day, special and regular holiday multipliers, contributions, and late and undertime rules, taken from HR’s own sheet.

    • Change a rule without touching a formula

  7. 07

    Employee records

    A sortable, editable table of every employee: rate, designation, contributions and app access.

    • Bulk actions and printable sign-in slips

  8. 08

    Cash advances and loans

    Advances and loans with balances, deducted automatically each cutoff.

    • Balance shown on the payslip
