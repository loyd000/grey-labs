The problem

before.

Most customers found Lampara on Facebook and asked the same questions in Messenger: how much will I save, what have you installed, when can you come?

The goal: answer those questions before the first message, and turn visitors into quote requests and booked site visits. Behind the site, an admin page lets the team manage projects, photos, the schedule and inquiries.

What was built

a walk through it.

  1. 01

    A clear first screen

    Licensing, the main promise and two actions: get a free quote, or calculate savings.

    • Built for phones first

  2. 02

    Savings calculator

    Enter a monthly electric bill and see the recommended system size, monthly savings and payback, with the assumptions stated plainly.

    • Quick-select common bill amounts
    • Leads straight into a quote request

  3. 03

    Real installations

    A portfolio of completed projects, filterable by residential, commercial and industrial, with photos for each.

    • Managed by the team from the admin page

  4. 04

    Book an installation date

    Customers see monthly availability and request a slot directly.

    • The team confirms from the admin page

  5. 05

    Answers before the first message

    Common questions on savings, brownouts, warranties and permits, then a free consultation request.
