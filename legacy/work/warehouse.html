Case study · Warehouse

every item, accounted for.

Lampara’s warehouse supplies every installation: panels, inverters, batteries, cables and tools. Materials went out to sites and did not always come back on paper. This module puts every item, request and release on record.

Client
Lampara Electrical Installation Services
Area
Warehouse and inventory
Platforms
Web and Android app
Used by
Manager, warehouse staff, engineers, admin

The problem

before.

Stock counts lived in a sheet that was always a few days behind. Engineers asked for materials by message, and releases and returns were hard to trace back to a project or a person.

The goal: know what is on hand, who asked for it, who released it, and what came back, without anyone keeping a separate list.

Who uses it

one system, every role.

  • Engineer

    Requests materials for a project, tracks the request, and returns what was not used.

  • Warehouse staff

    Receives deliveries, releases approved items, reviews returns and records serial numbers.

  • Manager

    Approves requests, adjusts stock with a stated reason, and checks accountability reports.

  • Admin

    Manages users and roles, contractor companies, and the monthly inventory report.

What was built

a walk through it.

  1. 01

    Stock by item and owner

    See on hand, reserved and available for every item, split by owner: the company or a contractor.

    • Low-stock balances flagged
    • Replenishment and reorder drafts

  2. 02

    Receive deliveries

    Log what arrived, from which supplier, against which PO, in one step.

    • Stock updates the moment it is recorded

  3. 03

    Engineers request a project’s materials

    Engineers pick items from the catalog to build the bill of materials for their project.

    • Search and filter the catalog by category

  4. 04

    Approve before anything leaves

    A manager approves each request. Approved items are reserved, so two projects cannot claim the same stock.

    • Full history on every request

  5. 05

    Releases and returns, on record

    Every release names who picked it up. Serial numbers are captured on release, and returns are reviewed before going back into stock.

    • Consumed versus returned, per project

  6. 06

    Every inverter serial, traceable

    Look up any serial number: when it was received, and which project it was released to.

    • Warranty claims start from the serial

  7. 07

    Accountability and reports

    Items out per person, a stock ledger and a monthly inventory report, ready for the owner.

    • Overdue items flagged with reminders
