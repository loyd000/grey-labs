Case study · Sales and projects

inquiry to after-sales.

Lampara sells and installs solar systems across Metro Manila and CALABARZON. Every job used to live in notebooks, Excel quotes and group chats. I built one system that follows each customer from the first inquiry to after-sales service.

Client
Lampara Electrical Installation Services
Industry
Solar installation, Philippines
Platforms
Web and Android app
Used by
Owner, office staff, field technicians

The problem

before.

Quotes were typed in spreadsheets, then typed again into contracts. Site inspections were done on paper and photographed into chats. Nobody could tell at a glance which customers were waiting for a survey, a quote or an installation.

The goal: one place for every customer, using the forms and steps the team already knew, so nothing had to be learned twice.

Who uses it

one system, every role.

  • Owner

    Sees the whole pipeline, approves quotes, and manages packages, document templates and team access.

  • Office staff

    Create leads, assign surveyors, schedule installations, and build quotes and contracts.

  • Field technician

    Gets assigned jobs on their phone and fills inspection reports and install checklists on site, even without signal.

What was built

a walk through it.

  1. 01

    See the whole pipeline at once

    Totals, the latest projects with their stage, and what is coming up this week, the moment you sign in.

    • Colour marks the stage, never decoration
    • Search everything with Ctrl K

  2. 02

    Start a project in one form

    A new lead takes a minute: contact details, property type and a real Philippine address.

    • Barangay, city or municipality, province
    • Duplicates are easy to spot from search

  3. 03

    The paper inspection form, now on a phone

    The site inspection report mirrors the printed form the surveyors already used: roof, mounting, electrical, photos per section.

    • Works offline, syncs when back online
    • Finishing it moves the project forward

  4. 04

    Quotes built from packages

    Start from a saved package, adjust items and prices, and keep every version. Approving a quote locks its numbers.

    • Versioned: v1, v2, v3 stay on record
    • Prices in pesos, totals always computed

  5. 05

    Payment terms that match the deal

    Pick the default schedule, a saved one, or write custom percentages for this customer.

    • The terms carry over into the contract

  6. 06

    Contracts generated from the approved quote

    Contract details, materials and payment terms come straight from the approved quote and print as a PDF. The signed copy is uploaded back.

    • One contract per quote version
    • No figure typed twice

  7. 07

    Installation checklist and sign-off

    Installers work through the QA checklist on site, in the team’s own wording, and collect the client’s signature.

    • Completion photos attached to the project

  8. 08

    After-sales and warranty

    Every customer keeps a service history, with warranty status on each ticket.

    • Issues tracked from report to resolved
