FAQ

good questions.

What kinds of systems do you build?+

Sales and project tracking, warehouse and inventory, and HR, attendance and payroll, on the web and on Android for your field team, plus websites and landing pages that bring customers in. If your team runs it on paper or Excel, it can be a system.

How do projects get started?+

With a free one-hour process session. Bring your forms and spreadsheets, and leave with a clear plan for your system.

How is pricing set?+

Every project starts with a fixed quote after the process review, with no open-ended billing. Start with one module, build a full system with a mobile app for your field team, and add monthly care if you want it.

Who owns the data?+

You do. It lives in your own database, not locked inside our product. Each person sees only what their role allows, enforced in the database.

What happens after launch?+

Staff training, then monthly care: hosting, fixes, support and small improvements. Changes are tried on a test copy first, so your live data is never at risk.
